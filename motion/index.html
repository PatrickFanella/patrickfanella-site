Motion set

Every page on the new site moves with these ten animations and nothing else. They share four durations and two curves. Each has a reduced-motion version: if your system asks for less motion, the page still shows everything, just without the movement.

120 ms
press, toggle
240 ms
hover, focus
420 ms
unfold, theme
620 ms
a letter landing, a page handing over

  1. Pour-in

    Letters drop into place from left to right. The moment each one hits, its ink splashes down from its foot and starts to pour.

    Trigger
    Page load
    Timing
    620 ms per letter, 60 ms apart, spring curve; ink splashes down as each letter hits
    Reduced
    Letters in place, ink already poured

    Ink

  2. Bleed

    Links and buttons let ink go from their bottom edge while you hover or focus them.

    Trigger
    Hover, keyboard focus
    Timing
    Fades in over 240 ms, out over 400 ms
    Reduced
    Underline and colour change only
    Cobalt Orange Gold

  3. Splash

    Pressing a button pushes the water out from your pointer and drops a little of that button's ink.

    Trigger
    Pointer down, Enter or Space
    Timing
    One solver step; the ink settles over about 2 s
    Reduced
    A 120 ms press only

  4. Stir

    The tank follows your pointer, and scrolling drags the water with the page.

    Trigger
    Pointer movement, scrolling
    Timing
    Continuous, 60 solver steps a second
    Reduced
    One still frame of poured ink

  5. Rise

    Rows lift into place as they scroll into view, here and in the selected work on the landing page. The browser runs it from the scroll position.

    Trigger
    Scrolling, CSS scroll-driven animation
    Timing
    Over the first 70% of the row's entry
    Reduced
    Rows already in place

  6. Glide

    A link to another place on the same page scrolls there instead of jumping, so you can see where you went. The scroll drags the ink along.

    Trigger
    Following a link within the page
    Timing
    The browser's smooth scroll, longer for a longer distance
    Reduced
    Jumps at once
    Glide back to Pour-in

  7. Unfold

    A row grows into its detail in place, and folds back the same way.

    Trigger
    Opening a row
    Timing
    420 ms, ease-out, view transition
    Reduced
    Opens at once
    Open this row

    Here's the detail. The browser takes a picture of the row before and after, then morphs one into the other.

  8. Hand-over

    Moving between pages, the old page drains downward while the new one fills in, and the name travels between its two places.

    Trigger
    Following a link on this site
    Timing
    620 ms, cross-document view transition
    Reduced
    The new page appears at once
    Go to the landing page

  9. Theme switch

    The other theme spreads out from the switch as a circle. The ground changes while the Ember smoke keeps its brick, amber and plum colors.

    Trigger
    The switch in the top bar
    Timing
    420 ms, ease-out, view transition
    Reduced
    Switches at once

  10. Focus

    The keyboard focus ring closes in on the element it lands on, so you can follow it.

    Trigger
    Tab, Shift+Tab
    Timing
    240 ms, ease-out
    Reduced
    The ring appears in place
    Tab to me Then me
