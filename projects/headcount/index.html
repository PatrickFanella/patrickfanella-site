Headcount

A Twitch time-series dashboard with continuous collection, replayable evidence and provisional viewer/chat pattern scores.

Status
Public research dashboard
Role
Design and engineering
Period
Active in 2026
Visit Headcount Repository

Product and scope

I built continuous Twitch collection, time-series storage, the read-only dashboard and explainable detectors. The hand-set detector weights are not calibrated; a high score identifies patterns for investigation, not proof that a streamer bought bots.

Stack
Go, PostgreSQL, TimescaleDB, TypeScript

The problem

A suspicious-looking audience chart needs context and retained evidence before it can support a conclusion.

What I built

  • Built channel polling, chat signals and per-stream time-series views.
  • Added versioned research detectors, replay context and a rate-limited read-only API.

How it fits together

Collection and scoring retain the source signals needed to inspect and replay an individual stream. The public dashboard has read-only access.

What I learned

  • A detector’s output should explain its evidence and limits instead of presenting an accusation as a fact.
