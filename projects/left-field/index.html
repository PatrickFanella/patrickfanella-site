Left Field

Seat-level election research combining public records, geography and provisional scores with visible source and publication versions.

Status
Public research application
Role
Design and engineering
Period
Active in 2026
Visit Left Field Repository

Product and scope

I built source-backed data intake, geographic queries and a publication model that keeps factual releases separate from provisional scoring. Missing inputs remain visible rather than becoming zeroes.

Stack
TypeScript, Next.js, PostgreSQL, PostGIS, Drizzle

The problem

A research score is difficult to assess when its sources, missing inputs or version cannot be inspected.

What I built

  • Built sourced seat-level records and geographic views.
  • Separated factual publication from versioned research scoring.

How it fits together

Published pointers and release contracts define which data readers see. Scoring identifies its model and factual inputs.

What I learned

  • Research priority is not a forecast. The interface should show the evidence and uncertainty behind a score.
