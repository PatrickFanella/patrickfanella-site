ytclip

A focused command-line wrapper for full YouTube downloads or precise timestamped excerpts.

Status
Command-line tool
Role
Design and engineering
Period
Active in 2026
Repository

Product and scope

ytclip wraps yt-dlp and ffmpeg behind a small command-line interface. Supply a video URL and optional start/end timestamps, choose audio-only when needed, and write the result to a selected output directory.

Stack
Shell, yt-dlp, ffmpeg, CLI

The problem

A short excerpt should not require remembering a long sequence of downloader and encoder flags.

What I built

  • Validated complete-video requests and bounded timestamp ranges.
  • Separated download, local encoding, and trimming commands.
  • Tested command construction with recording fakes for external media tools.

How it fits together

The wrapper delegates retrieval to yt-dlp and media processing to ffmpeg. External tool failures remain visible at the CLI.
