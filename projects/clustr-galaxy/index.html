Clustr Galaxy

A controller-first Unity prototype for exploring community graphs. Revision-pinned APIs and isolated local progress keep sessions consistent.

Status
Development prototype
Role
Design and engineering
Period
Active in 2026

Product and scope

Clustr Galaxy consumes Clustr’s bounded public graph payloads rather than connecting to its database. Explore provides navigation over loaded entities; Survey reveals compatible routes as signals are scanned. Sessions pin their graph revision and local progress stays in that revision’s namespace.

Stack
Unity, C#, Graph data, HTTP API, Local cache

The problem

A large community graph needs navigable scale, coherent local progress, and a useful response when live data is unavailable.

What I built

  • Built controller-oriented Explore and Survey modes over a community graph.
  • Pinned sessions to a graph revision and checked response compatibility.
  • Kept verified caches and authored demonstration data explicit in the loading flow.

How it fits together

The Unity client reads bounded HTTP snapshots, never the collection database.

Demo, offline cache, live transport, and broader platform qualification remain separate states.
