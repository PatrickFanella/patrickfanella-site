Overlaid

OBS overlays, a Twitch bot and an operator desk for live streams.

Status
Active development
Role
Broadcast tooling engineer
Period
2026
Repository

Product and scope

Overlaid is an operator desk for a stream: alerts, chat, ticker, goals, now-playing and starting, BRB and ending overlays for OBS, Twitch events fed into them, a chat bot with commands, timers and moderation, OBS scene controls, rehearsal sessions and a health page. It runs on your own machine beside OBS. It is in development with no public instance.

Stack
Go, TypeScript, React, PostgreSQL, OBS WebSocket

The problem

OBS needs a readable browser source for alerts, homelab status, and events without exposing an operator dashboard on stream.
